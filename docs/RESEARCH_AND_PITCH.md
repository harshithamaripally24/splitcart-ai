# SplitCart AI - Primary Research Dossier, Graphs & Competitive Defense

## 1. Primary Federal Reserve & CFPB Datasets (Direct Citations)

1. **Federal Reserve Board - FEDS Notes: "Buy Now, Pay Later" Beyond "Pay in 4", A Comprehensive Product Overview (June 5, 2026)**
   * **Primary Source Link:** https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/buy-now-pay-later-beyond-pay-in-4-a-comprehensive-product-overview-20260605.html
   * **Key Findings:**
     * U.S. BNPL originations reached **$156.7 Billion**, split evenly between 4-payment "Pay in 4" plans (**$78.3B**) and longer-term installment loans (**$78.4B**).
     * The share of BNPL loans carrying **0% APR fell from 83% (2020-2021) to 63%**, meaning **37% of BNPL volume now carries interest** when shoppers exceed single-cart Pay in 4 thresholds.

2. **Federal Reserve Board - Consumer & Community Context (August 2026) & SHED Microdata (May 2026)**
   * **Primary Source Link:** https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-august-consumer-community-context.htm
   * **Raw Dataset (CSV/Stata):** https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed_data.htm
   * **Key Findings:**
     * **18% of liquidity-constrained BNPL users** (households with <$100 buffer) **incurred a bank overdraft fee caused directly by an automated BNPL installment pull** colliding with recurring living expenses like rent.
     * **26% of BNPL users** reported making at least one late payment.

3. **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) - Consumer Use of Buy Now, Pay Later and Other Unsecured Debt (Jan 2025 / Dec 2025 Market Report)**
   * **Primary Source Links:**
     * https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_BNPL_Report_2025_01.pdf
     * https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_bnpl-market-report_2025-12.pdf
   * **Key Findings (Sample: 892,668 credit records / 335.8M BNPL loans):**
     * **63% of BNPL borrowers held multiple simultaneous BNPL loans** at the same time ("loan stacking").
     * **32% held simultaneous active BNPL loans across two or more providers**.
